Pricing & Payments

Wash & Fold items are weighed on certified digital scales once they arrive at our processing hub. You will receive a digital weight confirmation SMS and itemized breakdown before washing begins.

Doorstep collection and delivery are 100% free for all orders meeting the minimum order threshold of ₹249. A nominal logistics fee applies to orders below this amount.

We accept online payments via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), net banking, Apple Pay, as well as cash or card upon delivery.
