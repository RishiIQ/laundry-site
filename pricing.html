Pricing FAQs

Clear answers to all your billing, payment, and refund questions.

Wash & Fold is weighed on certified digital scales upon collection. You receive a digital weight confirmation SMS before processing starts so your invoice is always 100% transparent.

Doorstep pickup and delivery are completely free for all orders above ₹249. A nominal ₹40 logistics fee applies to orders below this amount.

We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), net banking, Apple Pay, and cash/card upon delivery.

If you are not 100% satisfied with the cleanliness or pressing quality of any garment, notify us within 24 hours and we will re-clean and press your item free of charge.
