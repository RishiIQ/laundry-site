Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026. Learn how LaundriX collects, protects, and handles your personal and location data.

Sections

  • Information We Collect
  • How We Use Data
  • Location & Pickups
  • Data Sharing
  • Security Standards

  • Contact Us

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, schedule a doorstep pickup, or interact with our digital platforms, we collect certain personal and technical information to fulfill our service commitments:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, verified email address, phone number, and encrypted password.
  • Pickup & Delivery Logistics: Physical address, landmark instructions, apartment/flat numbers, and scheduled time slot preferences.
  • Payment Information: Transaction identifiers and payment method tokens (we never store raw credit card numbers directly on our servers).
  • Device & Usage Diagnostics: IP address, device model, operating system, browser type, and interaction timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data strictly to facilitate dependable laundry processing and streamline your ordering experience:

  • To coordinate doorstep collections, automated routing for drivers, and delivery dispatching.
  • To send live SMS, WhatsApp, or in-app push notifications regarding garment cleaning stages and driver arrival times.
  • To generate accurate invoices, receipts, and process refunds if applicable.
  • To diagnose application bugs, improve routing efficiency, and enhance our digital booking interface.
Zero Selling of Personal Data LaundriX does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or address logs to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies for commercial profiling.

3. Location Information & Doorstep Pickups

To provide accurate pickup estimates and dispatch the nearest collection driver, we may request access to your device's precise or approximate location when scheduling orders. Location tracking is only active during the active pickup and delivery window.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We only share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us under strict non-disclosure and data protection agreements:

  • Payment Gateways: Certified PCI-DSS compliant payment aggregators (e.g., Stripe, Razorpay).
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Encrypted hosting and database providers operating within certified Tier-4 data centers.
  • SMS & Dispatch Partners: Telecommunication providers used exclusively to send order tracking notifications.

5. Security Standards

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is protected using 256-bit TLS (Transport Layer Security) encryption. Internal access to customer address records is strictly restricted to authorized dispatchers.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to your stored personal records, correct inaccurate information, or request full account and data deletion:

  • Access & Export: Request a copy of your historical order and address data.
  • Correction: Update your profile, phone numbers, and saved addresses directly in your account settings.
  • Deletion (Right to be Forgotten): Request the permanent removal of your account and related telemetry.

7. Contact Our Privacy Office

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is managed, please contact our Data Protection Officer:

  • Email: privacy@laundrix.com
  • Hotline: +91 98765 43210
  • Headquarters: LaundriX Technologies Inc., Indiranagar, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India
