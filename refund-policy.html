1. Our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee

At LaundriX, we take pride in delivering pristine garment care and crisp doorstep delivery. If you are not completely satisfied with the quality of your wash, fold, or dry cleaning, we are committed to making it right.

2. Free Re-Wash Policy

If you notice persistent stains that should have been removable or find any oversight in your returned garments, you are entitled to a free re-wash or re-press.

  • Reporting Window: Inspection claims must be reported within 48 hours of your delivery timestamp via our support app or hotline.
  • Condition: Garments must remain unworn, unwashed by third parties, and in their original delivered state.

3. Garment Damage & Loss Claims

While our master technicians exercise the utmost professional care, handling textiles involves inherent risks.

  • Inspection Upon Delivery: Any loss or damage must be documented and reported within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence.
  • Liability Limits: In accordance with industry standards, compensation for proven damaged or lost items is capped at a maximum of ten (10) times the processing charge for that specific item.
  • Exclusions: We cannot assume liability for items damaged due to hidden fabric defects, missing care labels, incorrect manufacturer tagging, or items left in pockets.
Transparent Compensation Approved refund or credit claims are processed directly back to your original payment method or credited instantly to your LaundriX wallet for future bookings.

4. Order Cancellations & Refunds

You may cancel an upcoming order at any time prior to driver dispatch without penalty:

  • Early Cancellation: Cancellations made 2+ hours before your scheduled pickup window receive a 100% full refund.
  • Late Cancellation: If our driver has already arrived at your designated pickup address, a nominal ₹150 logistics fee may be deducted from your refund.

5. Refund Processing Timelines

Once a refund has been approved by our quality team, the credit transfer is initiated immediately:

  • LaundriX Wallet Credits: Applied instantly (within 1 hour).
  • Credit/Debit Cards & UPI: Typically reflects in your bank account within 3 to 7 business days, depending on your financial institution.

6. How to File a Claim or Request Support

If you need assistance with an order adjustment, re-wash, or refund inquiry, please reach out to our customer advocacy team:

  • Email: support@laundrix.com
  • Support Hotline: +91 98765 43210
  • Hours: Monday–Saturday, 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM
